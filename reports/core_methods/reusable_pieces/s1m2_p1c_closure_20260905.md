## Exact computation

`src/sktlm/pieces/composed.py` implements one fixed-pass production engine.
For each grammar-licensed lexical form `u`, it generates the P0 transition
support directly from positions in `u`: every singleton, every contiguous
slice through `max_piece_length`, and the whole form. It computes the
restricted independent-boundary-prior normalizer, the piece-reweighted form
partition, conditional expected piece counts, segmentation entropy,
whole-form mass, all-singleton-path mass, and multi-piece mass with exact
forward/backward dynamic programming.

The engine does not import or instantiate `PieceLattice`. Lazy lexical spans
are generated on demand from P1b nodes; no persistent lexical-edge tuple is
built. Each span's exact marginalized form score enters the token DP, and the
token summaries enter the existing outer boundary-option/factor DP. Posterior
factor mass then composes lexical counts, conditional piece counts, internal
and visible boundary mass, and external-rule usage. Identity-only mass
marginalizes all legal inner piece segmentations of the outer identity
analysis, as required by the two-level model.

The resulting public segment summary includes:

- outer log partition and total posterior mass;
- lexical and reusable-piece expected counts;
- identity/latent mass and expected lexical tokens;
- boundary posteriors and external-rule expected usage;
- joint outer/inner entropy;
- deterministic work and bounded-cache counters.

## Shared state and finite bounds

The production engine is scoped to one immutable piece-scoring pass. A fresh
engine is required after every active-piece update. It shares per-form exact
summaries and piece scores through LRU caches with independent finite entry and
estimated-byte limits. An entry larger than its byte budget is evaluated but
not retained. The counters expose:

- lazy span traversals and unique candidate span hypotheses;
- factor, node, composed-state, and composed-transition work;
- piece-score requests, hits, misses, evictions, and oversize skips;
- form-cache hits, misses, evictions, and oversize skips;
- scorer/store lookups;
- current cache entries and conservative logical byte estimates.

Cache state affects only reuse and resource cost. Eviction or oversize bypass
cannot change support, order, weights, partitions, or expected counts.

## Oracle and substrate gates

The focused tests compare direct form DP with P0 at `rtol=1e-10` and
`atol=1e-12`, including single-symbol identity, multiple segmentations,
whole-form support beyond the normal slice bound, singleton atomization, and
repeated pieces across distinct forms. They compare the complete composed
kernel against the P1b materialization adapter plus the P0 `PieceModel` on:

- sandhi ambiguity and avagraha (`devo'pi`);
- visible-space evidence (`devas ca`);
- joined spelling (`devaśca`);
- a no-space continuous-like span (`tattvamasi`);
- an empty-grammar identity-only form;
- matched M0-prime/Devanagari phonology containing both lexical `/ai/` versus
  hiatus `a+i` and lexical aspirate versus plain consonant+`h`.

Compared quantities are per-form prior normalizer, form log score, conditional
piece counts, outer partition, lexical counts, composed piece counts,
identity/latent mass, expected lexical tokens, boundary posteriors, rule use,
and total posterior mass. Deterministic cache-bound/counter behavior is also
tested.
