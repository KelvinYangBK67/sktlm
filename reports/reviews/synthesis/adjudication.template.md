# Author adjudication template

Status: **template only; no review has been run**.

Create a milestone-specific adjudication only after 5/5 immutable raw reviews
and a separate synthesis exist. For every concern record the disposition
(accept, partially accept, reject with evidence, defer, require additional
analysis, require wording limitation, or require method change) and link the
response to result, code, specification, evidence, or limitation. Never edit
or replace a raw reviewer response.
