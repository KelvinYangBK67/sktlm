# Data licensing

## Project-authored materials

The Apache License 2.0 in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) covers project-authored source
code, configuration files, tests, and documentation.

## GRETIL source and derived text

GRETIL source texts and textual datasets derived from GRETIL are not covered
by the project's Apache License 2.0. This includes GRETIL-derived canonical
corpora and generated representation corpora.

When redistributed, the GRETIL-derived canonical and representation corpora
should be treated as licensed under the [Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International license][cc-by-nc-sa].
Redistributors must preserve appropriate attribution to GRETIL and the
applicable source texts, editions, and contributors; retain available
provenance; and clearly indicate modifications.

Nothing in this repository implies endorsement by GRETIL or any GRETIL
contributor. Users remain responsible for checking notices attached to the
underlying source editions and for complying with all applicable terms.
