## Project-authored materials

The Apache License 2.0 in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) covers project-authored source
code, configuration files, tests, and documentation.

## GRETIL source and derived text

GRETIL source texts and textual datasets derived from GRETIL are not covered
by the project's Apache License 2.0. This includes GRETIL-derived canonical
corpora and generated representation corpora.

license][cc-by-nc-sa]. To the extent that this project redistributes or adapts
GRETIL textual material, that material remains subject to the applicable
GRETIL license and any notices attached to the underlying source text or
edition.

Redistributors must preserve appropriate attribution to GRETIL and, where
provided upstream, the applicable source texts, editions, data-entry
contributors, and other credited contributors; retain available provenance;
and clearly indicate modifications.

Some tracked research audit and provenance reports under `reports/` contain
GRETIL-derived excerpts, candidate strings, or skipped source lines. Those
textual portions are not covered by the project's Apache License 2.0 and are
subject to the same GRETIL data-licensing terms described above. The surrounding
project-authored analysis, metadata, and software remain covered by the project
license as applicable.
